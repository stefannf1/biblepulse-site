Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026. Last updated: November 8, 2026.

Short version: your reading, notes, highlights, streak, and preferences stay on your device. We use one third party, Superwall, to run our subscription paywall — Superwall receives a limited, anonymous set of information described below. We don't sell your data and we don't share it with advertisers.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how BiblePulse ("BiblePulse," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application ("App") and this website ("Site"). BiblePulse is operated as an independent developer. You can reach us at BiblePulseApp@outlook.com.

For users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, BiblePulse is the data controller for personal data processed in connection with the App and Site.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information stored on your device only

The following stays on your phone and never leaves it unless you choose to export or share it:

  • Your reading position, chosen Bible translation, and reading streak
  • Your bookmarks, highlights, and personal notes
  • Your text-size, haptic, keep-awake, and theme preferences
  • Your daily reminder time, if you enable reminders
  • Your onboarding answers (season, biggest obstacle)
  • Your first name and church, if you enter them for prayer attribution

This data is kept in the App's local storage and, for the home-screen widget and Live Activity, in a protected shared container that only our App and widget can read. We do not have access to it. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

2.2 Information processed by Superwall (our paywall provider)

We use Superwall, Inc. to render our subscription paywall, run A/B tests, and measure conversion. When the paywall is loaded or interacted with, Superwall receives:

  • An anonymous device identifier generated by Superwall (not your Apple ID, email, or name)
  • Device model, operating system and version, locale, country, time zone, and app version
  • Paywall events (shown, dismissed, product selected, purchase started, purchase completed, purchase failed)
  • Your current subscription status (so the paywall isn't shown to active subscribers)
  • Approximate IP-derived location (country level), discarded after processing

Superwall processes this information under its own privacy policy, available at superwall.com/privacy. Superwall acts as our data processor; it does not sell this data or use it to serve third-party advertising.

2.3 Information processed by Apple and Google

When you subscribe, your payment is processed by Apple (through the App Store) or Google (through Google Play). We never see your payment card or bank details. We receive a signed receipt confirming the subscription is active, which is used to unlock premium features. Apple's and Google's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy respectively.

Crash logs and diagnostic reports may also be collected by Apple or Google at the operating-system level if you have opted in through your device settings. That data flows to Apple or Google, not to us.

2.4 Information we do not collect

  • We do not operate user accounts in version 1 of the App and have no way to identify you by name or email through the App
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Firebase Analytics, or Amplitude) outside of what is described above for Superwall
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, do not serve ads, and do not share any data with advertising networks
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites and do not request the App Tracking Transparency permission
  • We do not read the content of your notes, highlights, or bookmarks

3. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Provide the App's core features (reading, streak, notes, reminders)
  • Render, test, and measure the subscription paywall and win-back offer
  • Process your subscription and confirm your entitlement
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your information to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the App and the subscription you've purchased
  • Legitimate interests — to measure paywall performance, prevent abuse, and improve the App, provided our interests are not overridden by your rights
  • Consent — where required, such as for sending push notifications you've enabled
  • Legal obligation — where we must comply with applicable law

5. Sharing and disclosure

We share limited information only with:

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. If BiblePulse is ever involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, you will be notified in-app before any transfer, and the acquirer will be bound to terms at least as protective as this policy.

6. Data retention

  • On-device data is kept until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Superwall event data is retained by Superwall according to its own policy, generally up to 25 months, after which it is aggregated or deleted.
  • Support emails are retained for up to 3 years for service-quality and legal purposes.
  • Purchase receipts are retained as required for accounting, tax, and audit purposes (typically 7 years).

7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, including HTTPS for all network traffic, use of iOS and Android sandboxing for on-device data, and access controls on any support email we store. No system is perfectly secure; you use the App at your own risk.

8. International data transfers

Superwall is based in the United States. If you access the App from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, information processed by Superwall may be transferred to the United States. Such transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards. A copy of the safeguards can be requested at BiblePulseApp@outlook.com.

9. Children

BiblePulse is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent age of digital consent in your jurisdiction — 16 in some EEA countries, 13 in the US under COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact BiblePulseApp@outlook.com and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

10.1 Everyone

Because we do not operate user accounts, the primary way to exercise your rights over on-device data is through the App itself: you can view, edit, and delete notes, highlights, bookmarks, and preferences at any time, and uninstalling the App removes all local data.

To reset the anonymous Superwall identifier associated with your installation, delete and reinstall the App; a new anonymous identifier will be generated.

10.2 EEA, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to our processing
  • Receive your personal data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, email BiblePulseApp@outlook.com with your request. We will respond within one month.

10.3 California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to:

  • Know what categories of personal information we have collected, used, or disclosed
  • Delete personal information we have collected
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share for cross-context behavioral advertising)
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect sensitive categories)
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

In the preceding 12 months, we have collected the categories listed in Section 2, and disclosed them only to the recipients listed in Section 5. To exercise your rights, email BiblePulseApp@outlook.com. We may need to verify your request by asking you to confirm information you provided through the App.

10.4 Other jurisdictions

Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and other states with comparable privacy laws have similar rights of access, correction, deletion, and opt-out. To exercise them, email BiblePulseApp@outlook.com.

11. Cookies and the Site

This website is served statically through GitHub Pages and does not set first-party cookies, use analytics, or embed advertising. GitHub may collect limited standard server logs (IP address, user agent, request path) for operational and security purposes under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices, the App, or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where legally required or where the change materially affects your rights, provide notice in the App before the change takes effect.

13. Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints: BiblePulseApp@outlook.com.
