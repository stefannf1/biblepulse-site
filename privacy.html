Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version: your reading, your notes, your highlights, your streak — all of it lives on your phone and nowhere else. What we do collect (to run subscriptions and improve the app) is listed in plain English below.

What stays on your phone

Your streak, bookmarks, highlights, notes, chosen translation, reading position, text size, haptic preferences, and reminder times are stored locally on your device. We never send them to a server. If you delete the app, they go with it.

What we collect, and why

BiblePulse uses a service called Superwall to show subscription offers and run A/B tests on the trial screen. Through Superwall we receive:

  • An anonymous device identifier (not tied to your name, email, or Apple ID)
  • Basic device info: OS version, model, country, language, time zone
  • When a paywall is shown, dismissed, or purchased
  • Subscription status, so the paywall doesn't show if you've already subscribed

This is used to render the right offer and nothing else. We do not sell this data, we do not share it with ad networks, and we do not use it to track you across other apps.

When you subscribe, Apple handles the purchase and gives us a signed receipt confirming the subscription is active. We never see your payment details — only Apple does.

What we don't collect

We ship with no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising partners, no social-media trackers, and no account system. We can't read what you've highlighted, what notes you've written, or what you're reading — because that data never leaves your phone.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily verse reminder, your phone schedules the notification locally. Nothing about which verse, which time, or that you're using BiblePulse leaves your device.

Widgets and Live Activities

The Verse of the Day and Streak widgets read a small snapshot the app writes into a protected shared storage area on your phone. That snapshot stays on your device.

Crash reports and diagnostics

We do not collect our own crash reports or usage telemetry. Apple may collect basic crash diagnostics at the operating-system level if you've opted in with your device settings — that's between you and Apple, not us.

Children

BiblePulse is appropriate for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because we don't hold a profile tied to you, there's generally no personal data to export or delete on our side. If you want to reset Superwall's anonymous identifier, deleting and reinstalling the app will do that.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes materially — for example, if we add an opt-in account system or a new third-party service — we'll notify you in the app before anything new begins.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@biblepulse.app. We read everything.
