Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: open Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → tap BiblePulse → Cancel Subscription. Canceling during the 7-day trial will not charge you.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store subscriptions are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the charge, and request a refund. If you'd like us to follow up with Apple on your behalf, email us.

I lost my streak. Can you restore it?

Your streak lives on your phone, not on our servers, so we unfortunately can't restore it. The good news: streaks are intentionally forgiving — one missed day doesn't erase the habit, just the number.

Will my notes and highlights sync between devices?

Not yet. Your notes, highlights, and bookmarks are stored only on the device you made them on. Cross-device sync is on the roadmap.

Why am I being asked for review permissions?

Only after you've completed a reading plan do we ask whether BiblePulse is helping you. If you say yes, iOS may show its native review prompt. If you say no, we route you to email so we can fix what's broken.

Which Bible translation should I pick?

Any you're drawn to. We include public-domain classics (KJV, ASV, WEB) and permissively-licensed modern translations. You can switch anytime from Settings → Translations without losing your notes.

Does BiblePulse work without an internet connection?

Yes. The full text of every included translation is bundled with the app. You only need internet to download the app initially and to purchase a subscription.
