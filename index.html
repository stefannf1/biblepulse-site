BiblePulse

A quiet way to read the Bible, every day.

Offline. No ads. No feed. Just the Word, your streak, and a place to hold the verses that hold you.

What it is

A Bible app that remembers where you left off, keeps a gentle streak, and lets you highlight, bookmark, and keep notes on the verses that stay with you — all stored on your phone.

What it isn't

It isn't a social feed, a devotional-of-the-day treadmill, or a pay-to-read experience. Reading the Bible is free in every translation we ship.
